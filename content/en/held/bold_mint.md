Enemy shots take half a heart less (half at minimum), but you fire 8% slower.

One Pokémon on your team holds it, and it only works while that Pokémon is on the field. Fuse it from the Bag to make it work with the whole team. See [Held items](../mechanics/equipados.html).

## How it works
- Hits from enemy and boss projectiles take half a heart less, never going below half a heart. Shots that deal half a heart are not reduced.
- Contact, explosions and ground effects are not reduced.
- In exchange, the time between two of your shots is 8 % longer.

## Stacking
- A second copy does nothing more: the effect is either on or off. Having it held and fused, or held by two Pokémon of the team, is the same as having it once. The game won't let you fuse two alike, and new drops avoid the ones you already have.
- It adds up with [Metal Powder](metal_powder.html) (each takes off half a heart), but a hit never goes below half a heart.
