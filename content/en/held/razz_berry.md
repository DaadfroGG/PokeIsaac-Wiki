One in every ten shots deals four times the damage.

One Pokémon on your team holds it, and it only works while that Pokémon is on the field. Fuse it from the Bag to make it work with the whole team. See [Held items](../mechanics/equipados.html).

## How it works
- The game counts every projectile you fire while holding it; each 10th one deals four times its damage and is 35 % bigger. With several shots per volley, each projectile counts as one.
- The count carries over from room to room.
- Companion shots and child shots (split shots and the like) don't count.
- The ×4 is put on the shot itself when it is fired, so it is not limited by the ×3 cap on damage multipliers and does not show on the HUD.

## Stacking
- A second copy does nothing more: the effect is either on or off. Having it held and fused, or held by two Pokémon of the team, is the same as having it once. The game won't let you fuse two alike, and new drops avoid the ones you already have.
