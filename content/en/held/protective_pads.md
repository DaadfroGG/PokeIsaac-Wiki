Touching a normal enemy takes half a heart less (half at minimum).

One Pokémon on your team holds it, and it only works while that Pokémon is on the field. Fuse it from the Bag to make it work with the whole team. See [Held items](../mechanics/equipados.html).

## How it works
- When you are hurt by touching an enemy, you lose half a heart less.
- A hit always deals at least half a heart, so it only helps against enemies whose touch deals a full heart or more.
- It does not reduce boss contact, enemy shots, explosions or ground effects.

## Stacking
- A second copy does nothing more: the effect is either on or off. Having it held and fused, or held by two Pokémon of the team, is the same as having it once. The game won't let you fuse two alike, and new drops avoid the ones you already have.
- With [Metal Powder](metal_powder.html) as well (one held, one fused), a contact hit while standing still loses up to one heart, never going below half a heart.
