Hearts you pick up at full health turn into Pokédollars.

One Pokémon on your team holds it, and it only works while that Pokémon is on the field. Fuse it from the Bag to make it work with the whole team. See [Held items](../mechanics/equipados.html).

## How it works
- While your health is full, touching a [heart](../world/heart.html) on the floor turns it into 2 Poké Dollars, and a [half heart](../world/halfheart.html) into 1.
- Only red hearts lying in the room: soul hearts and hearts sold in the shop are not converted.
- Your wallet limit still applies.
- In co-op it doesn't convert while another living player is missing health, so the heart stays for them.

## Stacking
- A second copy does nothing more: the effect is either on or off. Having it held and fused, or held by two Pokémon of the team, is the same as having it once. The game won't let you fuse two alike, and new drops avoid the ones you already have.
