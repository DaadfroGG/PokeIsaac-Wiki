## League

The League runs on its own: one region per month, in this order: Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar, Paldea. Each gym is 6 waves of its type, then the leader's boss; an extra boss can show up between waves. After the 8 gyms come the Elite Four and the Champion. Completing it gives the region's «Champion» title.
