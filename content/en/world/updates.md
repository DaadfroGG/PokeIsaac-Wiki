What changed in each game update. Pages that describe something that changed have a **Changes** section at the bottom that says what the game did before.

## Version 1.2.0 (17 November 2026)

The wiki was checked against this version on 24 November 2026.

### Game modes
- **Dynamax Raid:** a weekly giant boss with one health bar shared by every player. See [Dynamax Raid](../mechanics/raid_dinamax.html) and its nine bosses.

### Combat and balance
- **Dual type:** you choose which type a dual-type Pokémon shoots with (T key or the HUD chip); the default picks the best type against each enemy. See [Dual type and pure type](../mechanics/tipos_dobles.html).
- **[X Accuracy](../items/m_precision_x.html)** no longer shoots by itself: it still locks onto the nearest enemy, but you have to press a fire key.
- **[Celestial Room](../rooms/angel.html):** alone you choose one of three wishes, weak items are swapped for better ones, and a soul heart always drops.
- **Infinite mode:** the [Bag](../mechanics/mochila100.html) gains 10 slots at every level, up to 150.

### Events, clans and market
These are not described on the wiki yet; this is what the game's update notes say.
- Automatic **daily and monthly events**, in addition to the weekly one: up to three at once, each with its own missions and final reward. There are 8 daily events that change every day (Type Day, Gold Day, Shiny Day, Boss Day and more) and 4 monthly events with an exclusive title.
- **Clan wars:** every Monday clans with similar points are paired up and whoever scores more during the week wins. Anyone who adds 50 points to their clan claims a reward the next week, with the title "War victor" if the clan won.
- **Market:** a weekly selection of 15 cosmetics that stay all week, plus a bundle with the 3 legendaries 25 % cheaper. Team Rocket brings a new shipment every 3 nights and, every night, a heist with a 40 % discount. 17 new animated skins.
- A new loading screen with tips.
