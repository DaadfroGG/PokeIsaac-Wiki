A Halloween treat that drops from enemies and pumpkins on Halloween floors.

Touch it to pick it up. In Classic mode, [Pumpkaboo](../pokemon/pumpkaboo.html) waits in the floor's first room next to a candy pot: walk up to the pot to trade your candies for a Halloween item drawn from the [super secret room](../rooms/supersecret.html) pool. The price goes up by 8 with each trade (10, 18, 26…).
