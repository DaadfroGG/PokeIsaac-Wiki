The boss of the weekly [Dynamax Raid](../mechanics/raid_dinamax.html) when it is Dynamax Dialga's week. Its health is shared by every player.

Everyone who dealt at least 300 damage when it falls earns the title "Dialga Tamer".

It uses the same attacks as the regular [Dialga](../bosses/dialga.html) boss, bigger and with the raid's rules.
