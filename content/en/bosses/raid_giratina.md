The boss of the weekly [Dynamax Raid](../mechanics/raid_dinamax.html) when it is Dynamax Giratina's week. Its health is shared by every player.

Everyone who dealt at least 300 damage when it falls earns the title "Giratina Tamer".

It uses the same attacks as the regular [Giratina](../bosses/giratina.html) boss, bigger and with the raid's rules.
