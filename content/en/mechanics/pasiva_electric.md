Electric shots can send a bolt to nearby enemies. The passive makes the bolt more frequent and stronger, and it reaches one more enemy at ranks 3 and 6.

## At rank 0
- Each hit has a 12 % chance to chain (18 % for a single-type Pokémon), +2 % per point of Luck.
- The bolt hits 1 other enemy within 180 px for 60 % of the shot's damage, with Electric type for the type chart.

## With rank
- +2 % chain chance per rank.
- The bolt deals 4 % more of the shot's damage per rank, up to 100 % at rank 10.
- The bolt reaches 1 more enemy at rank 3 and 1 more at rank 6.

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- Holding the [Zap Plate](../items/x_zap_plate.html) adds 2 ranks to this passive, if your Pokémon has this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
- "Single type" chances apply to Pokémon with only one type; dual-type Pokémon use the lower number.
