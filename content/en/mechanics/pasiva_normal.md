Normal Pokémon get luckier with each rank.

## At rank 0
- Normal has no extra effect on shots.

## With rank
- +0.35 Luck per rank. Luck raises the chance of status effects, critical hits and other rolls.

## Rank by rank
| Rank | Luck |
|---|---|
| 0 | +0 |
| 1 | +0.35 |
| 2 | +0.7 |
| 3 | +1.05 |
| 4 | +1.4 |
| 5 | +1.75 |
| 6 | +2.1 |
| 7 | +2.45 |
| 8 | +2.8 |
| 9 | +3.15 |
| 10 | +3.5 |

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- There is no Plate for this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
