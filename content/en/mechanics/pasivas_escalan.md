  value: Shoots faster and moves a little quicker.

  value: More luck (+0.35 per rank).

  value: More and stronger critical hits.

  value: More damage and, like Fire, burns more and spreads the flames.

  value: Charms more often; from rank 4, the charm makes them attack their own.

Each type's passive improves with your Pokémon's level: one rank every 8 levels (9, 17, 25... up to rank 7 at level 57) and, in the Infinite mode, up to 3 more ranks past floor 30. Each team member levels at its own pace (the bench a bit slower). With a dual type, each shot uses its own. Burns and poison now deal a percentage of YOUR damage, so they stay useful against the health of high floors. Your rank shows in the Bag, in your team panel.
