Psychic shots sometimes home in on enemies. The passive gives more homing shots and, from rank 4, confuses enemies.

## At rank 0
- Each shot has a 20 % chance to home in (30 % for a single-type Pokémon), +1 % per point of Luck.

## With rank
- A second roll, 4 % per rank, can also make the shot home in.
- From rank 4, each hit has a chance to confuse the enemy: 8 % at rank 4, +3 % per rank after that. A confused enemy wanders in random directions and doesn't attack for 1.5 s. Bosses can't be confused.

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- Holding the [Mind Plate](../items/x_mind_plate.html) adds 2 ranks to this passive, if your Pokémon has this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
- "Single type" chances apply to Pokémon with only one type; dual-type Pokémon use the lower number.
