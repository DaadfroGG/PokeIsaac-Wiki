Solo Classic mode only. From the Classic card you can switch on handicaps, the modifiers: each one raises the Pokédollars you earn during the run by its bonus (shown in brackets), and the bonuses add up, up to +140% with all six. Challenges are fixed sets of modifiers, some with a team rule; winning the run with one marks it as cleared.
