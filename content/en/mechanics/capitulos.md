Classic mode is split into chapters, like dungeon roguelikes: each has 3 floors and each floor comes out as one of its variants. Every floor ends with a boss and the last one of each chapter, with the chapter boss. From chapter 4 on there is no treasure room or shop and hits take a whole heart.
