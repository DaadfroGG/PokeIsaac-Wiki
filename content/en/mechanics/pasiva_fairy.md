Fairy shots charm enemies. The passive charms more often and, from rank 4, the charm can turn enemies against their allies.

## At rank 0
- Each hit has an 18 % chance to charm (27 % for a single-type Pokémon), +2 % per point of Luck. A charmed enemy is stopped for 0.8 s and slowed for 1 s; a boss is only slowed.

## With rank
- +2.5 % charm chance per rank.
- From rank 4, each hit has a chance to turn the enemy against its allies for 1.5 s: 4 % at rank 4, +4 % per rank after that. It attacks other enemies and doesn't hurt you on contact. Bosses can't be turned.

## Against bosses
- A charm only slows a boss for 1 s: it is never stopped and never turned against its allies.
- A slowed boss only walks at half speed between two attacks. Its attacks, dashes and the pause between attacks are not slowed at all.
- Against bosses, the rank only makes the slow more frequent (+2.5 % per rank).

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- Holding the [Pixie Plate](../items/x_pixie_plate.html) adds 2 ranks to this passive, if your Pokémon has this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
- "Single type" chances apply to Pokémon with only one type; dual-type Pokémon use the lower number.
