Balls give you companions that follow you and fight alongside you. You can have at most 3 at a time; the Apricorn Box raises the limit to 5. If you pick up another Ball with a full team, its companion waits in the Bag and comes out as soon as there is room (when you drop another). While you are at the limit, no more Balls show up.
