Balls give you companions that follow you and fight alongside you. They don't work like your own Pokémon: their damage only depends on the floor, they never use your stats or your shot effects, and they shoot on their own.

## In short
- A companion's damage is **2 + 0.4 per floor passed**, whatever your Damage stat. It grows as you go down.
- It **shoots on its own** at the nearest enemy, even when you don't shoot.
- Its shots are of **its own type** (Pichu: Electric, Togepi: Fairy…), not yours.
- Your **Fire rate, Range, Shot speed, Luck, multishot, homing, piercing and other shot effects don't apply** to it, and it **never lands critical hits**.
- Some of your items still boost its hits: see [What boosts companions](#what-boosts-companions).

## How you get them
- Each Ball you pick up gives **one companion**. Two copies of the same Ball give two companions.
- You can have **3 companions at a time**. The [Apricorn Box](../items/m_caja_bonguri.html) raises the limit to **5**.
- If you pick up a Ball with a full team, its companion **waits in the Bag** and comes out as soon as a slot frees up.
- While every player is at the limit, **no more Balls appear** in item rooms and shops.
- Companions that only last a room or a few seconds don't take a slot: the [Poké Ball](../items/pokeball.html) capture does, but the Joltik of [Lure](../items/a_colonia.html) and [Balm Mushroom](../items/m_seta_aroma.html), the Pidgey of [Plume Fossil](../items/d_fosil_pluma.html), the Magnemite of [Card Key](../items/a_llave_magnetica.html), the helpers of [Trainer Cap](../items/gorra.html), the companion of [Pokémon Box Link](../items/a_caja_pokemon.html) and the copies made by [Catching Pocket](../items/a_bolsillo_captura.html) don't. They all leave when you enter another room.
- In co-op each player has their own companions and their own limit. Companions follow and help the player who owns them.
- Enemies your companions kill count as your kills, for every item that counts kills (bosses never count for those items).
- Companions have no health: enemies can't hurt or kill them. Only the short-lived ones (Joltik, Pidgey, bees) disappear after a while or after a few bites.

## How they move and shoot
- They float about 34 px behind you, on the side opposite to where you last shot, and circle a little.
- A shooting companion targets the **nearest enemy within 380 px** and aims ahead of where it is going. Its shots fly at a fixed speed for **360 px**.
- Each one has its own fire interval (table below). Your fire rate doesn't change it.

## Damage
| Floor | 1 | 5 | 10 | 20 | 30 | 50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Damage per shot | 2 | 3.6 | 5.6 | 9.6 | 13.6 | 21.6 |

- This is the base of every shooting companion, and of the touch, laser and turret companions. Some companions multiply it (Igglybuff ×1.8, Gastly ×1.3).
- A captured enemy ([Poké Ball](../items/pokeball.html)) and the helpers of [Trainer Cap](../items/gorra.html) never go below this value either.
- [Exp. Share](../items/repartir_exp.html) **doubles** it.
- A few companions use **your Damage stat** instead (they are the exception): Misdreavus' aura, Phanpy's charge, Zubat's bite, Voltorb's explosion and the Joltik, Pidgey and bee bites. Their numbers are on each Ball's page.
- The [type chart](tabla_tipos.html) uses the companion's type: a Pichu does ×1.5 to Water enemies even if you are a Fire Pokémon.

## What boosts companions
These effects work on companion hits too:
- [Exp. Share](../items/repartir_exp.html) (×2 to the companions that use the floor damage).
- [Plates](../items/x_flame_plate.html) (×1.15) and Gems and Z-Crystals (×1.3), when the **companion's** type matches. A Fire Plate boosts your Magby even if you aren't Fire-type.
- [Expert Belt](../items/cinta_experto.html) (×1.3 on bosses), [Binding Band](../items/banda_atadura.html) (×1.3 on slowed enemies), the ×3 on a sleeping enemy, the +50 % on an enemy marked by the [Scanner](../items/escaner.html).
- [Big Root](../items/raiz_grande.html) heals you from their damage too.
- Held items that say so on their page, such as [Wiki Berry](../held/wiki_berry.html) and [Icy Rock](../held/icy_rock.html).

## What doesn't
- Your Damage stat and every Damage multiplier shown on the HUD.
- Fire rate, Range, Shot speed, size, multishot, homing, piercing, bouncing and every effect that changes your shots.
- Critical hits and everything that needs one.
- Effects that only trigger on "your shots" or on a shot's first hit: echoes, extra splits, combo counters…
- Luck and type passive ranks.

## Statuses
- A companion's shots can still apply **its own type's status** at the base chance: burn 15 % (Fire, Dragon), poison 20 %, slow 25 % (Ice), stun 15 % (Steel), charm 18 % (Fairy), lightning 12 % (Electric), knockback (Water, Rock, Ground).
- Luck, the single-type bonus and passive ranks don't raise these chances. Burns and poison from a companion are based on its own hit damage. See [Status effects](estados.html).
