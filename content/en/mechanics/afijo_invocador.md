A Summoner boss calls three wild Pokémon of its type each time it enters a new phase.

## Effect
- Most bosses change phase once, at 50 % health; mega bosses change twice, at 70 % and 35 %. Some bosses use their own thresholds.
- The three Pokémon are ordinary enemies of the boss's type, picked at random. They have 80 % of the health a normal enemy has on that floor.
- Summoned Pokémon give almost no experience to [your Pokémon](niveles_pokemon.html).
- With [Enraged](afijo_furioso.html), the boss starts in phase 2 without summoning: it only summons at its next phase, if it has one.

All affixes and which bosses get them: [Boss affixes](afijos.html).
