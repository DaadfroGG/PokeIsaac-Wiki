Ground shots are bigger and often push enemies back. The passive makes them bigger, stronger and pushier and, from rank 3, they can stun. [Rock](pasiva_rock.html) has the same passive.

## At rank 0
- Shots are a little bigger than other types' shots.
- Each hit has a 50 % chance to push the enemy back (bosses are never pushed).

## With rank
- +3 % damage per rank.
- Shots get a little bigger with each rank.
- +5 % push chance per rank (up to 90 %), and the push is 10 % stronger per rank.
- From rank 3, each hit has a chance to stun the enemy for 0.4 s: 4 % at rank 3, +2 % per rank after that. Bosses can't be stunned.

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- Holding the [Earth Plate](../items/x_earth_plate.html) adds 2 ranks to this passive, if your Pokémon has this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
