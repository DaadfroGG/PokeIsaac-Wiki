At the end of chapter 2, Mewtwo at the end of chapter 3 and every 10 floors of endless Ultra Space, a different shiny mega boss each run: it starts in its normal form, Mega Evolves in front of you at 70 % and enters its final phase at 35 %. Each species has its own mega attack. Double reward (two rounds of items and Mega Tickets).
