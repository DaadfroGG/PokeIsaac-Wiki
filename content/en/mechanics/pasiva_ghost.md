Ghost shots fly through rocks. The passive makes enemies flee from you more and more often.

## At rank 0
- Ghost shots pass through rocks instead of breaking on them.

## With rank
- Each hit has a 4 % per rank chance to scare the enemy: it runs away from you and doesn't attack for 1.5 s. Bosses can't be scared.

## Rank by rank
| Rank | Scare chance |
|---|---|
| 0 | — |
| 1 | 4 % |
| 2 | 8 % |
| 3 | 12 % |
| 4 | 16 % |
| 5 | 20 % |
| 6 | 24 % |
| 7 | 28 % |
| 8 | 32 % |
| 9 | 36 % |
| 10 | 40 % |

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- Holding the [Spooky Plate](../items/x_spooky_plate.html) adds 2 ranks to this passive, if your Pokémon has this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
