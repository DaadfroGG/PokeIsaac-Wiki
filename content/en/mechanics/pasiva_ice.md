Ice shots slow enemies. The passive slows more often and, from rank 3, freezes enemies that are already slow.

## At rank 0
- Each hit has a 25 % chance to slow the enemy for 1.5 s (37.5 % for a single-type Pokémon), +2 % per point of Luck.

## With rank
- +3 % slow chance per rank.
- From rank 3, hitting an enemy that is already slowed can freeze it, if that hit didn't slow it again. The chance is 6 % + 3 % per rank. A frozen enemy can't move for 1.2 s; a boss is slowed for 2 s instead.

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- Holding the [Icicle Plate](../items/x_icicle_plate.html) adds 2 ranks to this passive, if your Pokémon has this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
- "Single type" chances apply to Pokémon with only one type; dual-type Pokémon use the lower number.
