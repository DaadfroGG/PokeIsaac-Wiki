Pick a Pokémon, its level and your items to see the stats you would really have: the diminishing returns, level and shiny bonuses, type bonuses, fire rate in shots per second and the resulting damage per second. Share your build with the link button.

The planner is being extended: items that it cannot simulate completely yet are marked, and listed under the stats, so you always know what the numbers include. The rules it follows are described on [Diminishing returns](rendimientos.html), [Stats in practice](estadisticas_reales.html), [Critical hits](criticos.html) and [Item stacking](acumulacion.html).
