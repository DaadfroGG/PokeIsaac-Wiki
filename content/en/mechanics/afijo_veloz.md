A Swift boss moves faster and waits less between its attacks.

## Effect
- It moves 25 % faster (20 % faster for League bosses). Each new phase still makes it 12 % faster on top, like any boss.
- The pause between two attacks is 25 % shorter, so its attacks come one after another more quickly.
- Steelix and Mewtwo never get this affix.

All affixes and which bosses get them: [Boss affixes](afijos.html).
