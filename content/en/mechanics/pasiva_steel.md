Steel shots stun enemies. The passive stuns more often and, from rank 4, makes you take less damage from big hits.

## At rank 0
- Each hit has a 15 % chance to stun the enemy for 0.6 s (22.5 % for a single-type Pokémon), +2 % per point of Luck. Bosses can't be stunned.

## With rank
- +2 % stun chance per rank.
- From rank 4, any hit of a full heart or more hurts you half a heart less.

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- Holding the [Iron Plate](../items/x_iron_plate.html) adds 2 ranks to this passive, if your Pokémon has this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
- "Single type" chances apply to Pokémon with only one type; dual-type Pokémon use the lower number.
