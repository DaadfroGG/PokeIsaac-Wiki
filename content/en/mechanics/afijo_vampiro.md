A Vampire boss heals each time you lose health.

## Effect
- Each time your health (hearts and soul hearts) goes down, the boss gets back 2.5 % of its maximum health, never above full.
- It counts anything that costs you health, not only its own attacks. In co-op, it counts every player.
- It's one heal per hit, whatever its size: half a heart and two hearts cost you the same.
- League bosses never get this affix.

## Tips
- Avoiding small hits matters as much as avoiding big ones.
- Hold back items that cost health to use until the boss is dead.
