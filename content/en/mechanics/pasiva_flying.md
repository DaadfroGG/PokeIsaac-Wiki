Flying Pokémon get faster with rank and learn to dodge hits, up to 10 % of them.

## At rank 0
- Flying has no extra effect on shots.

## With rank
- +0.04 Speed per rank.
- 1.5 % per rank chance to dodge a hit, up to 10 % (reached at rank 7). Explosions can't be dodged. After a dodge you can't be hit for 0.5 s.

## Rank by rank
| Rank | Speed | Dodge chance |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | +0 | 0 % |
| 1 | +0.04 | 1.5 % |
| 2 | +0.08 | 3 % |
| 3 | +0.12 | 4.5 % |
| 4 | +0.16 | 6 % |
| 5 | +0.2 | 7.5 % |
| 6 | +0.24 | 9 % |
| 7 | +0.28 | 10 % |
| 8 | +0.32 | 10 % |
| 9 | +0.36 | 10 % |
| 10 | +0.4 | 10 % |

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- Holding the [Sky Plate](../items/x_sky_plate.html) adds 2 ranks to this passive, if your Pokémon has this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
