A mode with 18 towers of 30 floors, one per type. You climb with a team of that single type, made only of basic Pokémon and without legendaries. Clearing a tower gives 2,000 tokens and 5 Gold Tickets; you can replay it, but the prize is only given once per type. The Type Tower has its own ranking, and cleared towers count for the [milestones](hitos.html).
