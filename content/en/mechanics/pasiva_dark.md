Dark Pokémon land critical hits on their own. The passive gives more and stronger critical hits.

## At rank 0
- Dark shots have a 10 % critical-hit chance (15 % for a single-type Pokémon), +1 % per point of Luck.
- A critical hit deals ×2 damage (more with some items).

## With rank
- +1.5 % critical-hit chance per rank.
- +0.1 to the critical-hit multiplier per rank (×2 becomes ×3 at rank 10).

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- Holding the [Dread Plate](../items/x_dread_plate.html) adds 2 ranks to this passive, if your Pokémon has this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
- "Single type" chances apply to Pokémon with only one type; dual-type Pokémon use the lower number.
