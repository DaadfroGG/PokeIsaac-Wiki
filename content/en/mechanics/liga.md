The League changes region every month, going through Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Sinnoh, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar, Paldea, each with its own gym leaders, Elite Four, Champion and badges. The Ranking starts from zero every season. Completing the League earns the «Champion» title of that season's region; players who had completed it before have «Kanto Champion».

Every region's gyms, with their enemies and bosses: [Floors and chapters](../world/floors.html#league).
