A weekly fight against a giant boss with a single health bar shared by every player. Everyone's hits count toward the same bar, and what is left carries over between attempts.

## How it works
- The boss changes every week: one of the [nine Dynamax bosses](#the-bosses).
- You get 3 attempts a day, alone or in co-op. In a room, the host starts the raid and each player has their own build and their own attempt. With no attempts left you can still play with your room, but your damage doesn't count.
- An attempt lasts 4 minutes or until you faint.
- Your Pokémon (the one picked in the menu) enters fully evolved and at level 15.
- Waves of enemies of the boss's type arrive during the fight, and a Gigantamax Pokémon shows up from time to time: beating it gives 1 Mega Ticket (once a day).
- A boss is summoned a couple of times (a mega boss about 2 minutes 30 into the attempt: 2 Mega Tickets, once a day). Beating one breaks the raid boss's shield: it is stunned for 4 seconds and takes 50 % more damage for 10 seconds.

## Your build
Before each attempt you build your team's items with **20 points**. The game offers 12 items that change every week and are the same for everyone: 3 basic ones (2 points each), 4 of the next tier (4 points), 3 more (6 points) and 2 of the strongest (9 points). They are attack, stat, shot and defence items; each can be taken once.

## Dynamax
You have a Dynamax gauge. It fills with the damage you deal (1,500 damage fills it) and also by itself, in about 90 seconds. When it is full, press your active item button: for 12 seconds you grow and deal **double damage**, and you are healed 2 hearts (ignoring the healing limit) and gain 2 soul hearts.

## Rewards
- **If the boss falls:** everyone who dealt at least 300 damage gets 1,500 tokens, 10 Mega Tickets, 10 Golden Tickets and the boss's title (for example "Arceus Tamer"). If it survives the week, those players get 300 tokens.
- **Community milestones:** when the shared health reaches 75 %, 50 % and 25 %, everyone who hit it gets a reward.
- A weekly ranking shows who deals the most damage.
