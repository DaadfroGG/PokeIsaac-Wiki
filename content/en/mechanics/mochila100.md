## In Infinite mode
The limit goes up by 10 for every Infinite level (the levels of the [endless mode](modo_infinito.html)), up to 150: 110 on floors 31-40, 120 on 41-50, 130 on 51-60, 140 on 61-70 and 150 from floor 71. In Infinite the limit goes up: 10 more every 10 floors, up to 150."

You can carry up to 100 passive items at once. With a full Bag you cannot take another: drop one first in the Inventory (it stays on a pedestal in case you change your mind). The active item does not count, since it is swapped. In the co-op loot draft, whoever has a full Bag skips their turn.

## Changes
- **1.2.0 (17 Nov 2026):** in Infinite mode the Bag gets bigger as you go down (see above). *Before:* the limit was 100 passive items everywhere, including Infinite.
