Bug Pokémon shoot faster and move a little quicker with each rank.

## At rank 0
- Bug has no extra effect on shots.

## With rank
- The time between shots is 1.2 % shorter per rank.
- +0.02 Speed per rank.

## Rank by rank
| Rank | Time between shots | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | ×1 | +0 |
| 1 | ×0.988 | +0.02 |
| 2 | ×0.976 | +0.04 |
| 3 | ×0.964 | +0.06 |
| 4 | ×0.952 | +0.08 |
| 5 | ×0.94 | +0.1 |
| 6 | ×0.928 | +0.12 |
| 7 | ×0.916 | +0.14 |
| 8 | ×0.904 | +0.16 |
| 9 | ×0.892 | +0.18 |
| 10 | ×0.88 | +0.2 |

## Raising the rank
- In Classic and the League, your rank goes up by one every 8 levels (rank 1 at level 9, rank 7 at level 57). See [Scaling type passives](pasivas_escalan.html) for the other ways to raise it.
- Holding the [Insect Plate](../items/x_insect_plate.html) adds 2 ranks to this passive, if your Pokémon has this type. The highest rank is 10.
- A dual-type Pokémon alternates its two types, one shot in two. Each shot uses the passive and the rank of its own type.
