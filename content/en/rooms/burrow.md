A burrow hidden under a normal room. When you clear that room the ground shakes and a mound opens: step on it to go down to a whack-a-mole game. Shoot the Diglett (+1) and Dugtrio (+3) as they pop out of their nine holes and avoid the Voltorb (-3). The more points, the better the prize.
