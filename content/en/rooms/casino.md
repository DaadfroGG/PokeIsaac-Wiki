The game room: two slot machines (1 Pokédollar per spin) and two vendors. Pikachu asks for 1 Pokédollar and Mimikyu for 1 heart per try: stand on the plate in front of them until the ring fills. They usually give pickups (Mimikyu's are better) and sometimes an item; each try raises the item chance and, once they give it, they leave. You may also find one of them alone in a normal room.
