- label: Where
  value: Door with a red “X”; needs no keys. Appears in ≈20% of floors from the 3rd, never two in a row. The battle starts when you get close; if you don’t want it, you don’t have to.

  value: 2 Pokémon in a row (3 from chapter 3)
- label: Health of its Pokémon
  value: Half the health of a floor boss each
- label: Between one Pokémon and the next
  value: Recalls it, talks and calls the next one
- label: The rival’s prize
  value: Boss item, heart or key and Pokédollars

  value: '0'

A human rival waits for you in a dueling circle and challenges you to a battle of 2 Pokémon in a row (3 from chapter 3). Each one is a normal floor boss with half its health; between them the rival recalls it, talks and throws another Poké Ball. It does not count as a floor boss: no trapdoor and no Mega Tickets. When beaten, the rival gives up and leaves a boss-loot item, a heart or a key and Pokédollars.
