An ancient chamber with a crystal pillar and five plates carved with Unown runes. The pillar shows an order: step on the plates in the same order. If you get it right, a treasure rises from the floor (an item with 0-1 mistakes, a golden chest with 2, a chest with 3). If you get it wrong, Unown awaken (2-3) and on the 3rd mistake you lose half a heart. The sequence is 3, 4 or 5 plates depending on the floor. You can always leave.
