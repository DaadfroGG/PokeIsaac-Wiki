Fire rate ++, range -

## How it works
- Fire rate: shortens the delay between two shots by 0.05 s. The fire-rate bonuses of all your items are added up: the first 3 points count in full, the next 4 at half and the rest at a quarter. Whatever you do, you can never shoot more than 12 times per second.
- Range −80 px: subtracted from how far your shots travel, in full. Range can never drop below 120 px.

## Stacking
- Every copy adds its bonuses again, with no limit on copies. The diminishing returns described above apply to the total, not to each copy separately.
