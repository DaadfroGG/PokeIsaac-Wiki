Your Fire-type shots deal 30 % more damage; Damage +0.3

## How to get it
- It is never found in treasure rooms, shops or boss rooms. The only way to get it is to beat a **zone guardian** of the Fire type: the mega boss on the 10th floor of each zone of [endless Ultra Space](../mechanics/zonas_infinito.html).
- Guardians of this type appear in the Ashen Crater, and sometimes in The Void (which can bring a guardian of any type).
- It is only offered if someone on the team has a Fire-type Pokémon (bench included). If you already have it, the [Firium Z](x_firium_z_bag.html) takes its place.
- The guardian leaves three pedestals and you keep only one: the other two disappear.

## How it works
- The +30 % is applied when a Fire-type shot hits, so it does not show in your Damage stat on the HUD. It is not limited by the ×3 cap on damage multipliers.
- A dual-type Pokémon only gets it on its Fire shots, one shot in two.
- Burn and poison ticks don't get the +30 %.
- It multiplies with the [Flame Plate](x_flame_plate.html): ×1.3 × 1.15 ≈ ×1.5 on Fire shots.
- The Damage +0.3 is a normal stat bonus: it shows on the HUD and follows the [diminishing returns](../mechanics/rendimientos.html).

## Stacking
- It does not stack with the [Firium Z](x_firium_z_bag.html): both give the same +30 %, so holding the two is still ×1.3. The Z-Crystal then only adds its critical hits.
