Your companions' shots and hits deal double damage

## How it works
- Your [companions](../mechanics/companeros.html)' shots and contact hits deal twice as much damage.
- It is applied to the companions' damage, not to yours: your Damage stat doesn't change, and it is not limited by the ×3 cap on damage multipliers.
- It doesn't double the Joltiks of the [Balm Mushroom](m_seta_aroma.html).
- In Classic and League modes, where Pokémon gain levels, the Pokémon on your bench get 70 % of the experience from your kills instead of 35 % (see [levels](../mechanics/niveles_pokemon.html)).

## Stacking
- A second copy does nothing more: still ×2.
