Damage +0.5

## How it works
- The +0.5 Damage is a flat bonus: it adds up with your other flat bonuses and follows the [diminishing returns](../mechanics/rendimientos.html) (in full up to +3 in total, then less and less).

## How to get it
- Treasure rooms, boss rewards and shops.
- In Classic mode, the shop of the last floor of each chapter (every 10th floor) also sells two vitamins (more expensive than usual items): HP Up first if someone is below the heart limit, then two at random among Protein, Calcium, Zinc, Iron, PP Max and Carbos, preferring ones you don't have yet.

## Stacking
- Everything stacks: each copy adds its stats again (with the diminishing returns on Damage).
