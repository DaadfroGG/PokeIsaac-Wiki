Flying-type enemies deal you less damage; half a heart more

## How to get it
- It is never found in treasure rooms, shops or boss rooms. The only way to get it is to beat a **zone guardian** of the Flying type: the mega boss on the 10th floor of each zone of [endless Ultra Space](../mechanics/zonas_infinito.html).
- For now no mega boss is Flying-type, so no guardian can drop this item: it cannot be obtained yet.
- Unlike the Gem and the Plate, it is always offered, even if nobody on the team has that type (as long as you don't already have it).
- The guardian leaves three pedestals and you keep only one: the other two disappear.

## How it works
- It works on hits from a Flying-type enemy and its shots, after the type chart.
- A hit of 1 heart or more costs you ½ heart less.
- A hit of only ½ heart has a 50 % chance to do nothing at all.
- The +½ heart of max HP works as soon as you pick it up, whatever the enemy.

## Stacking
- You can only hold one copy: the guardian does not offer it again once you have it.
