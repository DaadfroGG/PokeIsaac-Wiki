Boosts the Flying type: if your Pokémon is Flying-type, its type passive goes up 2 ranks and its shots of that type deal +15% damage

## How it works
- It only works if the Pokémon you are playing has the Flying type. The Plate can appear as soon as anyone on the team has a Flying Pokémon, bench included, but a Pokémon without that type gets nothing from it.
- A dual-type Pokémon only gets the bonus on its Flying shots, one shot in two.
- The +15 % is applied when a Flying shot hits, so it does not show in your Damage stat on the HUD. It is not limited by the ×3 cap on damage multipliers.
- The +2 ranks go to the [Flying passive](../mechanics/pasiva_flying.html). They don't change the Damage stat either.
- Burn and poison ticks don't get the +15 %.

## Stacking
- It does not stack: a second copy adds nothing (still +2 ranks and +15 %).
