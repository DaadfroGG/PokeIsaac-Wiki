At the start of each fight, much more speed and 25% more fire rate for 4 seconds

## How it works
- Each time you walk into a room that still has enemies, for 4 seconds you get Speed +1.4 and the time between your shots is multiplied by 0.8 (25 % more shots per second).
- The timer starts when you enter, not when the enemies appear.
- The fire rate limit still applies: never more than 12 shots per second.
- It multiplies with the [Power Herb](hierba_unica.html): with both, the time between shots is ×0.48 during those 4 s.

## Stacking
- A second copy does nothing more.
