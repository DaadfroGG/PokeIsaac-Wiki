You shrink: harder to hit and speed +

## How it works
- Your Pokémon shrinks to 80 % of its size. The area where enemy shots and enemies can hit you shrinks with it, so attacks miss you more often.
- The hitbox has a minimum size: very small Pokémon don't shrink below it.
- Speed +0.4, always on.
- With the [Big Mushroom](seta_grande.html) (×1.2), both sizes multiply: ×0.96, almost your normal size.

## Stacking
- A second copy does nothing more: still ×0.8 and +0.4.
