Your Normal-type shots deal 30 % more damage; +8 % critical hit chance

## How to get it
- It is never found in treasure rooms, shops or boss rooms. The only way to get it is to beat a **zone guardian** of the Normal type: the mega boss on the 10th floor of each zone of [endless Ultra Space](../mechanics/zonas_infinito.html).
- No zone has this type as its theme, so only The Void (which can bring a guardian of any type) can bring one.
- It is only offered if someone on the team has a Normal-type Pokémon (bench included). There is no Normal Gem in this set, so it is offered directly.
- The guardian leaves three pedestals and you keep only one: the other two disappear.

## How it works
- The +30 % works like the Gem's: applied when a Normal-type shot hits, not shown on the HUD, not limited by the ×3 cap. Dual-type Pokémon only get it on their Normal shots. Ticks don't get it.
- The +8 % critical chance works on all your shots, whatever their type. It adds to your other [critical hit](../mechanics/criticos.html) sources and to Luck (+1 % per point).

## Stacking
- A second copy adds nothing to the +30 %.
- The +8 % critical chance adds up with other Z-Crystals: two Z-Crystals of different types give +16 %.
