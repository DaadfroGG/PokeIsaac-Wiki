Your damage ×1.3, but every hit you take costs half a heart more.

## How it works
- Your Damage is multiplied by ×1.3 permanently. It is a damage multiplier like the others: it goes through the [diminishing returns](../mechanics/rendimientos.html) (full up to ×2.3, then a quarter, never above ×3).
- Every hit that actually damages you takes an extra half heart (a hit worth half a heart costs a full heart, a hit worth a heart costs one and a half). This applies to every source: enemies, shots, explosions, hazards.
- Hits that are dodged or blocked completely (for example by a mirror-style dodge) cost nothing, so there is nothing to add.
- It is a cursed item: it is offered in the Distortion Room.

## Stacking
- A second copy does not stack: still ×1.3 and still +½ heart per hit.
