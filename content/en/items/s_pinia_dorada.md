Luck +2

## How it works
- Luck +2: added to your Luck. Luck counts in full up to 10; above 10 only a quarter counts. It raises the chance of drops from enemies and of a reward when you clear a room, the chance of critical hits (+1 % per point) and the chance of type-passive status effects (+2 % per point).

## Stacking
- Every copy adds its bonuses again, with no limit on copies. The diminishing returns described above apply to the total, not to each copy separately.
