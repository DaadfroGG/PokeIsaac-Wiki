Your Electric-type shots deal 30 % more damage; +8 % critical hit chance

## How to get it
- It is never found in treasure rooms, shops or boss rooms. The only way to get it is to beat a **zone guardian** of the Electric type: the mega boss on the 10th floor of each zone of [endless Ultra Space](../mechanics/zonas_infinito.html).
- Guardians of this type appear in the Observatory, and sometimes in The Void (which can bring a guardian of any type).
- It is only offered if someone on the team has a Electric-type Pokémon (bench included), and only once you already hold the [Electric Gem](x_electric_gem.html): until then the guardian offers the Gem instead.
- The guardian leaves three pedestals and you keep only one: the other two disappear.

## How it works
- The +30 % works like the Gem's: applied when a Electric-type shot hits, not shown on the HUD, not limited by the ×3 cap. Dual-type Pokémon only get it on their Electric shots. Ticks don't get it.
- The +8 % critical chance works on all your shots, whatever their type. It adds to your other [critical hit](../mechanics/criticos.html) sources and to Luck (+1 % per point).
- The +30 % multiplies with the [Zap Plate](x_zap_plate.html): ×1.3 × 1.15 ≈ ×1.5 on Electric shots.

## Stacking
- The +30 % is the same bonus as the [Electric Gem](x_electric_gem.html)'s: holding both is still ×1.3.
- The +8 % critical chance adds up with other Z-Crystals: two Z-Crystals of different types give +16 %.
