Treasure rooms open without a key

## How it works
- When you walk into a room, every locked door of that room that leads to a treasure room opens by itself, and you keep your key.
- It only opens treasure room doors. Other locked doors and golden chests still need a key (for golden chests, see the [Big Pearl](perla_grande.html)).
- In co-op it works for the whole team as long as one player carries it.

## Stacking
- A second copy does nothing more.
