Every track in the game, where it plays and who made it. All of it comes from free music packs on OpenGameArt:

- [Full 8-bit JRPG Soundtrack](https://opengameart.org/content/full-8-bit-jrpg-soundtrack-downloadable-and-editable-music-pack) by **Blake Speers**, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): every track except one.
- [15 Melodic RPG Chiptunes](https://opengameart.org/content/15-melodic-rpg-chiptunes) by **Aureolus_Omicron**, under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): the game over theme.
